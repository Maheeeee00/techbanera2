FAQ

Questions clients ask us

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll reply within one working day.

How long does a website take?

A business website takes 2–3 weeks. An e-commerce store takes 3–5 weeks, depending on products and payment options.

Does the POS work without internet?

Yes. Sales continue offline and sync to the cloud automatically when the connection returns.

How soon will SEO show results?

Most clients see ranking movement in 6–8 weeks and steady traffic growth within 3–6 months. You get a report every month.

What budget do I need for ads?

We recommend at least $150–$300 per month in ad spend so campaigns gather enough data to optimise.

Do I own my website and code?

Yes. Once the project is paid, you own the website, code, domain and all content.
